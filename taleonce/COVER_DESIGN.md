# Cover art direction

The five PNG illustrations in `public/assets/covers/` were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Each prompt asked for a vertical 2:3 premium literary cover illustration, refined painterly texture, a calm dark lower quarter for the site's HTML title overlay, and no generated text, logo, or watermark.

| Story | Final scene prompt | Palette |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Too Late to Love Me* | Claire in an ivory dress walks away from a distant gilded ballroom through palace doors toward dawn; an abandoned wedding ring rests on a marble balustrade. Elegant, bittersweet, emotionally decisive. | Oxblood, ivory, muted gold, dawn blue |
| *The Winter Boundary* | A pack cartographer with rolled map and lantern stands on a snowy pine ridge; glowing boundary beacons lead toward distant homes and a subtle wolf silhouette watches among trees. Cold mystery turning toward belonging. | Midnight teal, frost blue, silver, amber |
| *The Last Name on the Register* | A determined night archivist in a rain-lit Gothic archive; an open ledger with illegible handwriting and a red ribbon sits in the foreground, with a distant shadowed vampire figure. Intelligent noir mystery. | Plum, charcoal, blood red, brass |
| *The Apartment Above the Laundromat* | A glowing washer and rain-wet street frame a mother and adult daughter folding clothes together in their small laundromat. Intimate resilience after a flood. | Deep teal, amber, cream, rain blue |
| *The Map of Unmade Roads* | An apprentice mapmaker draws on a parchment map at a cliffside table; a luminous winding road rises from the paper across a mountain valley as older paths fade into mist. Wonder with moral weight. | Indigo, copper, parchment gold, violet |

Titles are rendered by the website, keeping spelling correct and allowing the art to be reused at different sizes.
